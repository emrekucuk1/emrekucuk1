About Me

I am a senior software developer focused on backend architecture, cloud infrastructure and end-to-end product delivery. At Qoolize I work across several roles at once — Project Manager, Backend Developer and DevOps Engineer — leading a team and taking products from design to production.

	•	Designing and maintaining multi-tenant B2B and B2C platforms for the eSIM and telecom market
	•	Building API-first services with NestJS and .NET, and modern frontends with Next.js and Vue
	•	Running self-hosted infrastructure: containerized deployments, CI/CD and observability stacks
	•	Background in embedded systems and IoT (STM32, PIC, ESP32, MQTT)
